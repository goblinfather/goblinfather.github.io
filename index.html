ENGINEERING NOTES / NOV 2026

Building proves feasibility. Evaluation tests effectiveness.

My biggest lesson from ReconGuard was the difference between a functioning prototype, validated security performance and actual user value.

01 — Architecture decisions

Separate LLM observation extraction from deterministic policy evaluation. Validate quotations and speaker attribution; maintain an organization-level exposure history instead of making unsupported identity claims. Preserve unassessed states when analysis fails.

02 — What the prototype demonstrated

Four synthetic interactions were processed using recorded live Meta API results. Regression testing covered verification, policy behavior, retries and replay. The risk sequence reproduced as designed; one detailed fragment expectation did not match. This is not a generalized accuracy estimate.

03 — What is not yet established

Real-world detection accuracy, false-positive rates, live contact-center integrations and operator effectiveness remain unvalidated. The prototype does not establish that attacks are prevented or that workload is reduced.

04 — Next research questions

Expand independent evaluation data, compare keyword and LLM baselines, measure evidence grounding, latency and cost, and test whether operator guidance improves decision quality and reduces unnecessary burden.
